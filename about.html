About

Economy and current affairs, explained plainly

Explained by Ishan Gautam is a YouTube channel and companion site covering the Indian economy and current affairs — monetary policy, growth and inflation data, the budget, and the events that move markets and policy. The goal is straightforward: take a release or a headline that sounds complicated, and explain what it actually means and why it matters.

Videos go up on YouTube as explainers and breakdowns. This site carries the longer written analysis behind them — the data, the sourcing, and the context that doesn't always fit into a ten-minute video.

Where the analysis comes from

Every piece here is built on official data and reporting — releases from the Reserve Bank of India, the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, and established financial press — read closely and explained in plain language. Nothing here is investment advice; it's analysis meant to help you understand the economy, not a recommendation to act on.
