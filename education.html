About the Certifications

CIA

Certified Internal Auditor

The CIA, awarded by the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA), is the only globally recognised certification for internal auditors. It's built around three parts — covering the foundations of internal auditing, practice of internal auditing, and business knowledge for internal auditing — and is widely treated as the benchmark credential for anyone building a career in internal audit, risk or governance.

For audit professionals, it signals a working command of internal control frameworks, risk-based auditing and governance standards that employers and audit committees recognise on sight.

CISA

Certified Information Systems Auditor

The CISA, awarded by ISACA, is the leading certification for professionals who audit, control and assess an organisation's information systems. The exam covers five domains spanning IS audit process, governance, systems development, IT operations, and protection of information assets.

As more of internal audit's work touches IT systems, data and cyber risk, CISA has become a natural complement to a traditional audit background rather than a niche add-on.
